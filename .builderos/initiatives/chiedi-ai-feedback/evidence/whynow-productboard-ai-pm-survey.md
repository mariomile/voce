"We partnered with independent research firm UserEvidence to survey 379 product professionals from enterprise organizations with 500+ employees."

"Every product team we surveyed is using AI tools. Not 99%. 100%."

"More striking: 96% report using AI consistently, with nearly half describing it as 'deeply embedded' into their workflows."

"Among product professionals specifically, 94% use AI daily or often. Zero respondents said they don't use it at all."

"When asked which skills are becoming increasingly important, product professionals selected an average of 3.5 options. The top four: Data literacy (58%), Synthesizing customer insights (54%), Systems-level thinking (53%), Strategic thinking (52%)."

"Product professionals report saving an average of 4 hours per task with AI, totaling approximately 33 hours across their core functions."

"88% use two or more different AI models."

"While 100% of respondents use AI tools, only 65% say their company has a documented AI policy."

Nota: fonte primaria datata (Productboard x UserEvidence, pubblicata 23 ottobre 2025, n=379 professionisti di prodotto in aziende enterprise 500+ dipendenti): il 54% cita "synthesizing customer insights" come competenza sempre più critica nell'era AI e il 94% usa AI quotidianamente o spesso, prova diretta che i PM oggi usano assistenti AI in modo routinario, incluso per sintetizzare insight dai clienti. Nota metodologica: il campione è enterprise (500+ dipendenti), non startup/scaleup italiane; da citare con questo caveat.
