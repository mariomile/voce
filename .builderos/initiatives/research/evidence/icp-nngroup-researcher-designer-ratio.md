# icp-nngroup-researcher-designer-ratio

**Class:** doc
**Captured:** 2026-09-28 · By: Claude Code (problem-framer, ricerca web) · Where: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ux-developer-ratio/

Publication date: articolo NN/G "Typical Designer-to-Developer and Researcher-to-Designer Ratios", dati raccolti nel 2020 ("As part of a recent survey to understand the state of UX and design teams..."; il paragrafo di sintesi apre con "In 2020, the most typical researcher-to-designer-to-developer ratio reported was 1:5:50").

"Summary: In 2020, the most typical researcher–to–designer–to–developer ratio reported was 1:5:50. Beware, however, of using role ratios alone to measure teams' maturity or impact."

"Though the survey had 557 respondents, only 377 people responded to questions about the number of researchers, designers, and developers at their companies; this article is based on their answers. (These questions were optional.) Survey respondents were design-community members (80% identified design or research as their primary role) from a variety of industries, working at companies of various sizes."

"There was also variability in the reported researcher–to–designer ratio, and similar encouraging patterns emerged: About half of respondents (54%) reported having at least 1 researcher for every 10 designers, and only 8% reported having 1 researcher for every 50 designers or more."

Nota: fonte storica di riferimento nel settore UX per il rapporto ricercatore/designer/developer (1 ricercatore ogni 5 designer ogni 50 sviluppatori), n=377 su un campione di 557 rispondenti, comunita di design (80% con ruolo primario in design o ricerca). Utile come base per stimare quanti PM/designer non hanno accesso a un ricercatore dedicato, ma il dato ha 5-6 anni (raccolto nel 2020) e NN/G stessa avverte di non usare i rapporti da soli come indicatore di maturita: fonte autorevole ma dato datato, da trattare come riferimento storico/debole per il 2026, non come stato attuale del mercato.
