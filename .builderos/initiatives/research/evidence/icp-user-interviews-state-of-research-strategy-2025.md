# icp-user-interviews-state-of-research-strategy-2025

**Class:** doc
**Captured:** 2026-09-28 · By: Claude Code (problem-framer, ricerca web) · Where: https://www.userinterviews.com/state-of-research-strategy

Publication date: "Published: October 22, 2025" indicato in fondo alla pagina, sezione Methodology.

"Research has achieved scale: Eighty-seven percent of survey respondents report having dedicated Researchers (UXRs) at their companies."

"Democratization expands alongside dedicated Researchers: Seventy-one percent of respondents said their organization has people who do research (PwDR) but aren't dedicated Researchers. Of those who have a PwDR presence, about half of respondents (48%) said that they have fewer than 10 PwDR at their company and one third said they have more than 20 PwDR."

"Supporting research consumes significant time: One third of researchers (including ReOps professionals) spend up to 9% of their time supporting PwDR, and 53% spend up to a quarter of their time supporting dedicated Researchers."

"From July 25 to August 9, 2025, we collected 485 qualified responses via social media, our weekly newsletter (Fresh Views), and an in-product slideout; we posted the survey in research-related groups on LinkedIn and Slack; and members of our team and friends within the UX research community shared the survey with their own professional networks."

Nota: report State of Research Strategy 2025 di User Interviews (parte del filone State of User Research), n=485 rispondenti prevalentemente UXR/ReOps (79% con titolo User/UX Research). Il dato chiave, 71% delle organizzazioni ha "people who do research" (PWDR) non dedicati, con quasi meta di queste che ne hanno meno di 10, e supporta bene la tesi che il discovery non-dedicato (PM, designer) e diffuso. Fonte primaria con metodologia dichiarata: forte, ma il campione e composto perlopiu da chi ha gia un titolo di ricerca (bias verso chi lavora ancora in UXR, i rispondenti disoccupati sono sotto-rappresentati per esplicita ammissione del report gemello 2025 State of User Research).
